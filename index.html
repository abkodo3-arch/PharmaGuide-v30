Upload a clear prescription photo

Medical OCR mode uses image enhancement, multiple OCR passes, medication-dictionary matching, fuzzy spelling correction, and prescription-pattern parsing. Handwriting can still be misread, so every drug, strength and dose must be verified before dispensing.

Checking AI OCR…
🤖 AI Medical OCR: The AI reader analyzes the handwriting visually, then matches each likely drug against the app medication dictionary and returns ranked possible medication matches. It does not invent unknown medicines or silently guess missing doses.
Best accuracy: upload the prescription itself, straight-on and close-up. If the prescription is only a small part of a larger photo/screenshot, crop it first. The reader will show possible medication matches rather than dumping raw OCR garbage.
